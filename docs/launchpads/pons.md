## Creator reward model
**v2: paid in the quote asset, never the launched token.** 1% base fee → 30% protocol / 70% creator (with buyback on: 30/35/35); creator tax 0–10% → 100% creator; snipe tax distributed like the base fee. Post-graduation launch-token-side fees are sold back into the pool (≤3% price impact) before crediting, so conversion creates bounded sell pressure. Optional creator buyback vests over 5 years. Fees accrue until swept, then sit in FeeEscrow for `claim()`/`claimToken()`; `transferCreatorFeeRecipient` and a "route fees to holders" distributor exist. Escrow snapshot $635.5K unclaimed across 61 assets (174.4 ETH, 139.5 NVDA, 32.6 SPY, 24,006 USDG…). **v1: partly in the launched token** (70/30 active, 90/10 legacy), claimable from the locked V3 position or auto-routed by Pons automation.
